What is Power Bull?

Power Bull is a reliable backup power solution designed to keep essential devices like WiFi routers, modems, and security cameras running during power outages. It supports 12V equipment and is also compatible with 9V devices.

How to Use It?

Simply plug the Power Bull adapter into a 220V AC power source, then connect the output to your device (such as a router, modem, or camera). It will automatically provide backup power when the main supply is interrupted.
